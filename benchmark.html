AI Answer Evaluation

Benchmark

Public-copy blind evaluation registry. The copy is evaluated without using candidate identity or reference marks to influence the AI score.

Scoring Protocol

BLIND
Marks are based on answer demand, content accuracy, coverage, structure, dimensions, examples/data, conceptual clarity, introduction, conclusion and presentation. Diagrams, maps and flowcharts are audited separately. Blank or unattempted answers receive zero. This benchmark is independent and does not imply endorsement by any institute.
